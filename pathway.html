YOUR PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

System Administrator Career Pathway

Career progression varies by experience, certifications, organization, and the skills you build.

01
ENTRY LEVEL

IT Support / Help Desk Technician

Provides first-line technical assistance to users and helps solve common hardware, software, and account issues.

Main responsibilities

  • Respond to user support requests
  • Set up computers and user accounts
  • Record issues and solutions

Skills and experience

Customer supportTroubleshootingComputer basics
02
INTERMEDIATE

Junior / System Administrator

Maintains servers, user access, system updates, backups, and day-to-day IT infrastructure.

Main responsibilities

  • Manage users and permissions
  • Monitor servers and services
  • Perform backups and updates

Skills and experience

Linux / WindowsNetworkingScripting
03
ADVANCED

Senior Administrator / Systems Engineer

Designs, improves, and troubleshoots complex systems while helping ensure performance, reliability, and security.

Main responsibilities

  • Plan infrastructure improvements
  • Handle complex incidents
  • Automate and document processes

Skills and experience

Advanced securityCloud systemsLeadership
